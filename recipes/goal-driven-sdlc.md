Use the stages to navigate complex delivery, not as a mandatory sequence. For simple questions or small changes, skip planning and other stages that do not help. Follow explicit dependencies and profile permissions. The runtime enforces safety and coordination invariants, while this workflow describes practices agents can adapt to the task.
